Quick Answer

Price Controls 231 explains how Oman Electricity Transmission Company earns its regulated revenue. You learn how the Authority for Public Services Regulation (APSR) sets Maximum Allowed Revenue from operating costs, capital costs and the weighted average cost of capital, how it assesses grid investment for efficiency, and how incentives apply to spinning reserve.

Key takeaways

  • OETC is the sole licensed transmission and dispatch operator in Oman, so regulation, not competition, sets its revenue.
  • Maximum Allowed Revenue is built from operating costs, capital costs and a return set by the weighted average cost of capital.
  • APSR assesses strategic grid investment for prudence and efficiency.
  • Performance incentives apply to spinning reserve and system reliability.

What will you be able to do after the course?

You will be able to read an OETC price control and explain where each part of the allowed revenue comes from. The course is built around three outcomes.

  • Explain the governance. How OETC is licensed and regulated under Oman's Sector Law.
  • Break down the revenue. How Maximum Allowed Revenue is calculated from capital costs, operating costs and the cost of capital.
  • Judge the incentives. How spinning reserve incentives work, and where regulation is heading on digitalisation, resilience and performance metrics.

What do the key terms mean?

These are the terms the course uses most, in plain English.

Term Meaning
OETC Oman Electricity Transmission Company, the sole licensed transmission and dispatch operator in Oman.
APSR Authority for Public Services Regulation, the body that regulates Oman's electricity sector and sets OETC's price control.
Maximum Allowed Revenue The revenue OETC is allowed to recover under its price control, abbreviated to MAR.
Building blocks The method of adding up efficient operating costs, capital costs and a return on capital to reach allowed revenue.
WACC Weighted average cost of capital: the blended return that debt and equity investors require, used to set the return block.
RPI-X A price control that lets revenue rise with retail price inflation minus an efficiency factor, X.
Spinning reserve Generating capacity that is running and synchronised to the grid, ready to respond quickly if supply is lost.
